## Summary of Changes

### 1. Material 3 Interactive Choice Cards
- Replaced the clunky HTML `<select>` dropdown with interactive, tactile Android Material 3 Choice Cards for competitive exams (SSC, Railways, State PSC, Banking, General Studies).

- Removed the floating card box with desktop margins on mobile, making the canvas edge-to-edge from the Android status bar (`env(safe-area-inset-top)`) to the gesture bar (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`).
- Preserved desktop beauty on larger screens with centered elevated container.

### 3. Thumb-Friendly Bottom-Pinned Action Bar
- Re-anchored the primary "Continue to Dashboard" button to a sticky bottom bar with gesture bar clearance.

### 4. Android Hardware Back Button Handling
- Integrated `@capacitor/app` `App.addListener('backButton')` to minimize the app gracefully on hardware/gesture back presses during onboarding.
