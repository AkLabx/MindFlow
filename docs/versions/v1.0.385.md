## 4. Engineering Decisions (Path A vs Path B)
### Decision 1: Local Timing Snapshot Cache, Bookmark Store Sync & Free-Tier Auth Hardening (`DEC-0035`)
- **Context & Problem Statement**: `mock_test_attempts.answers` strips `__cbt_meta` on completion (`DEC-0021`), `PreparedMockResultPage` lacked per-section time/negative-marking breakdowns and 1-tap bookmarking, `AuthPage` treated Supabase's obfuscated `identities: []` response as a new signup, `ResetPasswordPage` allowed 6-char passwords, and `#/reset-password#access_token=...` double-hash URLs dropped recovery tokens.
- **Path A (Alternative Considered)**: Add a new `time_spent_by_question` DB column/migration or leave `__cbt_meta` inside `mock_test_attempts.answers`; disable email confirmation or build custom SMTP edge functions.
- **Path B (Path Chosen)**: Keep `mock_test_attempts.answers` clean (`__cbt_meta` stripped) and store per-question timing in dedicated local/IndexedDB snapshots (`mindflow_mock_attempt_timing_*`) consumed by `computePreparedMockSubjectBreakdown()`, wire `Solutions` bookmarking through `useBookmarkStore` + `db` + `syncService.removeBookmark`, and harden standard Supabase Free Plan Auth (`buildAuthRedirectUrl`, `extractAuthHashParams`, `identities.length === 0` handling, `MIN_PASSWORD_LENGTH = 8`, `mindflow_is_signup`, and `SettingsPage` OAuth password linking).
- **Do-Not-Break Invariant**: Never leave `__cbt_meta` inside submitted `mock_test_attempts.answers`; keep `<TestLeaderboardCard />` exclusive to `PreparedMockResultPage` `Leaderboard` tab; preserve `MIN_PASSWORD_LENGTH = 8` across `AuthPage`, `ResetPasswordPage`, and `SettingsPage`; keep `extractAuthHashParams()` compatible with both `#/#access_token=...` and `#/reset-password#access_token=...`.
- **Decision ID Registered in Ledger**: `DEC-0035` (`OLD-025`).
