## 4. Engineering Decisions (Path A vs Path B)
### Decision 1: Zero-DDL Mock Tests Security Sign-Off & Gen 2 Advisor Backlog Triage
- **Context & Problem Statement**: Running Supabase `get_advisors(type="security")` and `get_advisors(type="performance")` during Gen 1B sign-off confirmed zero `ERROR`s and full `mock_*` RLS/RPC compliance, while surfacing non-blocking `WARN`/`INFO` lints (including `chat_room_messages` / `chat_message_reactions` `auth_rls_initplan` and unindexed FK `INFO` lints).
- **Path A (Alternative Considered)**:
  - Description: Author an unscheduled SQL DDL migration inside `v1.0.380` touching `mock_*`, `chat_room_messages`, `chat_message_reactions`, and unrelated AI/telemetry worker RPCs.
  - Why Rejected: Violates Gen 1B's out-of-scope boundary and Circuit Breaker #3 (destructive/unrequested DDL during a consolidation sign-off) while mixing Community Chatroom tables into the Mock Tests sign-off PR.
- **Path B (Path Chosen)**:
  - Description: Complete `v1.0.380` with **zero DDL changes**, formally sign off on `mock_*` RLS (`5/5` `rowsecurity = true`), `"Public read for leaderboard if completed"` (`USING (false)`), `get_mock_test_leaderboard` / `get_test_leaderboard` / `is_admin` (`search_path = public, pg_temp`, `anon_exec = false`, `auth_exec = true`), and 6 index coverages, while queuing the non-blocking `WARN`/`INFO` backlog (`multiple_permissive_policies` on `mock_*`, `anon` `EXECUTE` on 5 non-mock AI/telemetry worker RPCs, and `chat_room_messages` / `chat_message_reactions` `auth_rls_initplan` + 2 unindexed FKs) into Gen 2 Step 1 (`v1.0.381`).
  - Why Chosen / Evidence: Preserves strict `v1.0.380` stability, protects production DB state under Circuit Breaker #3, and hands Gen 2 an exact, prioritized SQL hardening spec.
- **Do-Not-Break Invariant**: Never reopen cross-user `SELECT` on `mock_test_attempts`, never grant `anon` `EXECUTE` on `get_mock_test_leaderboard` / `get_test_leaderboard` / `is_admin`, and never strip `SET search_path = public, pg_temp`.
- **Decision ID Registered in Ledger**: `DEC-0028`

### Decision 2: Tactile/Keyboard-Accessible Result & Series Controls + `/admin/*` Hierarchical Back
- **Context & Problem Statement**: Mobile `390x844` and accessibility audits caught missing `KeyboardAwareBottomBar` / `scroll-mb-28` / `44x44px` controls / `<NativeDialog>` `h2` & close button on `PreparedMockResultPage`, negative `-m-*` spacing + non-keyboard `<motion.div onClick>` cards + missing `<ArrowLeft />` on `TestSeriesPage`, generic `/dashboard` fallback for `/admin/*` hardware back in `useHardwareBackButton.ts`, and wall-clock date drift in `examPreparedMockTests.test.tsx`.
- **Path A (Alternative Considered)**:
  - Description: Leave `<motion.div onClick>` series cards and raw `fixed bottom-0` result footer untouched as "desktop-acceptable", and adjust test fixture dates manually.
  - Why Rejected: Violates `DEC-0002` (logical back), `DEC-0007` (accessible Top-Layer `<dialog>`), and `DEC-0008` (non-negotiable Android native `>=44x44px` tactile feel and keyboard avoidance), and leaves date-sensitive tests brittle across calendar days.
- **Path B (Path Chosen)**:
  - Description: Standardize `PreparedMockResultPage` on `<KeyboardAwareBottomBar fullWidth>`, `scroll-mb-28`, `min-h-[44px]`/`min-w-[44px]` `active:scale-95` `touch-manipulation` `aria-pressed` controls, `lang="hi"` Hindi blocks, `<h2 id="quick-jump-palette-title">` + explicit `44x44px` close `<button type="button">` inside `<NativeDialog>`, and validated `location.state?.returnTo`/`from`; upgrade `TestSeriesPage` with `min-h-[100dvh] w-full pb-28 scroll-mb-28`, visible `<ArrowLeft />`, and semantic `<button type="button">` series cards; route `/admin/*` hardware back to `ROUTES.ADMIN.ROOT`; and pin `Date` in `examPreparedMockTests.test.tsx`.
  - Why Chosen / Evidence: Verified `0` sub-44px buttons on `TestSeriesPage`, `PreparedMockResultPage`, and `AdminQuestionsPage`, `0px` horizontal overflow on Desktop + Mobile, and `275/275` deterministic Vitest passes.
- **Do-Not-Break Invariant**: Keep `PreparedMockResultPage` wrapped in `<KeyboardAwareBottomBar>`, preserve `lang="hi"` on Hindi review blocks and explicit `<NativeDialog>` headings/close buttons, keep `TestSeriesPage` series cards as semantic `<button type="button">` elements (zero `-m-*` negative wrappers), preserve `/admin/*` $\rightarrow$ `ROUTES.ADMIN.ROOT` fallback, and pin `Date` in schedule-dependent unit tests.
- **Decision ID Registered in Ledger**: `DEC-0028` (`OLD-017` retired)
