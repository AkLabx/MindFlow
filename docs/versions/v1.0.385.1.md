## 2. Executive Summary
Hotfix polish release `v1.0.385.1` automating the post-signup Email Verification experience and resolving the `"App Experience Paused / Reload App"` + Sign-In redirect loop reported when opening Supabase confirmation links from email clients (such as Gmail on Android). Eliminated the false `document.referrer.includes("android-app://")` standalone PWA check in `index.html`, extended auth link hydration timeouts in `supabase.ts`, expanded `useAppBootstrap.ts` to handle `#access_token`, PKCE `?code`, `?token_hash`, and redirect errors cleanly with `BroadcastChannel` notification, replaced jarring full-page cross-tab reloads in `AuthContext.tsx` with silent session hydration, and upgraded `AuthPage.tsx` with a dedicated `verify-email` auto-detection step.

## 4. Engineering Decisions (Path A vs Path B)
### Decision 1: Automated Dual-Tab Email Verification Flow & Android App-Referrer Fix (`DEC-0036`)
- **Context & Problem Statement**: Clicking Supabase's confirmation link in Android Gmail triggered `index.html`'s `#desktop-mode-warning` (`"Reload App"`) via `document.referrer.includes("android-app://")`, `replaceState` dropped `hasOAuthHash()` causing `getSession()` to time out at `1500ms` and bounce back to Sign In, and the original signup tab stayed stuck on the filled form.
- **Path A (Alternative Considered)**: Keep the inline green banner inside the signup form and force users to manually reload/sign in after clicking email links.
- **Path B (Path Chosen)**: Restrict `checkDesktopMode()` strictly to true PWA standalone (`display-mode: standalone` / `navigator.standalone`), protect auth link hydration with `mindflow_auth_hydrating` (`6000ms` timeout) + PKCE/`token_hash`/error handling, sync tabs smoothly via `BroadcastChannel('mindflow-auth-sync')` + `hydrateCrossTabSession()`, and transition `AuthPage` into an automated `step = 'verify-email'` screen that auto-signs in across both same-browser and cross-browser/device confirmations without penalizing brute-force rate limits.
- **Do-Not-Break Invariant**: Never include `document.referrer.includes("android-app://")` in `index.html`'s standalone PWA check; never increment `recordFailedAttempt()` on `'Email not confirmed'` during automated verification checks; keep `mindflow_auth_hydrating` guarded in a `try/finally` block in `useAppBootstrap.ts`.
- **Decision ID Registered in Ledger**: `DEC-0036` (`OLD-026`).
