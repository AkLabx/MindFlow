- **Entry Prerequisites:**
  - Verified Gen 1B graduation baseline (`v1.0.380`), `0` TypeScript errors, clean `vite build`, passing `vitest` and Playwright E2E smoke suite.
  - Gen 2 Step 1 (`v1.0.381`) completed on `feat/v1.0.381-student-home-mock-hub` (`PR #1125`) establishing the simplified student shell, 300-Q studio/builder caps, and `<InfoHintPopover />` foundation.
  - Gen 2 Step 2 (`v1.0.382`) completed on `feat/v1.0.382-mock-shell-pyq-domains` (`PR #1126`) delivering dedicated sub-page headers, the Paid-App 5-tab footer + `/purchased` hub, MWG scroll-snap swipeable tabs & `.mf-h-rail` card rails, `PYQ` vs `MOCK`/`EXPERT` taxonomy (`107` official BPSC & SSC CGL PYQ tests), and 6-domain exam grouping.
- **Exit / Graduation Criteria (`v1.0.390`):**
  - Distraction-free student navigation across Home (`/dashboard`), Custom Quiz Studio (`/mcqs`), Mock Test Series Hub (`/mcqs/test-series`), My Purchase (`/purchased`), Quiz History (`/quiz/library`), Saved Questions (`/quiz/bookmarks`), and Profile (`/profile` with unique Student ID).
  - Complete Paid Mock Test Courses lifecycle: Admin course/pricing packaging, student catalog & demo-locked previews, secure server-verified purchase & receipt cards (`My Purchase`), offline cached entitlement hydration, modular exam-authentic CBT interfaces (`BPSC Fixed`, `SSC CGL TCS iON`, `SSC CGL Eduquity`), and section/solution analytics.
  - Native Android WebView feel (`>=44x44px` `type="button"` `active:scale-95`, `<KeyboardAwareBottomBar>`, `scroll-mb-28`, MWG `<dialog>` / `popover="auto"` Top-Layer primitives) verified across Vitest, Playwright E2E, Supabase Security/Performance Advisors, and Chrome DevTools MCP (`0` console errors, `0px` horizontal overflow).
- **Strictly Deferred / Parked Scope (Do NOT Pull Into This Generation):**
  - Community Study Chatrooms & Social Feed hardening (superseded/deferred per `OLD-018`; relocated to `SidePanel` per `OLD-019`; do not pull into Gen 2 unless explicitly requested by the user).
  - Gemini Live Voice Coach `AudioWorklet` enhancements and institutional multi-tenant educator portals (parked for Gen 3).

  - `DEC-0001` (Canonical Exam Registry), `DEC-0002` (Logical Back), `DEC-0003` (State Ownership Matrix), `DEC-0004` (0ms Offline-First Dexie), `DEC-0005` (Option B Launch & OAuth Hash), `DEC-0006` (No Guest Mode), `DEC-0007` (MWG Top-Layer & `<16ms` INP), `DEC-0008` (Native Android Feel & `>=44x44px`), `DEC-0014`–`DEC-0028` (Admin CMS & Prepared Mock Tests invariants), `DEC-0029` (Student-First UX Simplification & Contextual `!` Guidance), `DEC-0030` (Paid-App 5-Tab Shell, `DedicatedPageHeader`, MWG Scroll-Snap Rails, `PYQ` vs `MOCK` Schema & 6-Domain Exam Taxonomy), `OLD-018`, and `OLD-019`.
